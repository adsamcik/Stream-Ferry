01

The short version

Stream Ferry does not operate an account system, analytics service, advertising network, telemetry pipeline, or developer database.

The app handles only the information needed to connect to services and devices you choose. App data remains on your Android device unless you explicitly send it to your Jellyfin server, your selected TV, or another app through Android’s Share action.

02

Data the app handles

Depending on the features you use, Stream Ferry may handle the following data on your device:

⌁

Jellyfin connection data

Your server address, user profile information, encrypted access token, and an app installation identifier used when communicating with Jellyfin. Your password is used only to sign in and is never stored.

▦

Media and playback data

Library metadata, artwork caches, watch state, local playback history, resume positions, queue state, and optional offline downloads.

▰

TV and network data

Local device names, addresses, identifiers, capabilities, route preferences, and compatibility results needed to find and control a TV.

▧

Local video access

Files or folders you select through Android’s system picker, plus device video metadata if you separately choose to allow full video-library access.

!

Diagnostics

Redacted crash reports and event logs are kept in app-private storage. They never leave your phone unless you explicitly share them.

03

Where data goes

Stream Ferry does not send data to the developer. Information moves only as needed for actions you request:

1

Your Jellyfin server receives sign-in requests, library requests, playback reporting, watch-state changes, and download requests. Its operator controls that server and its privacy practices.

2

Your selected TV or receiver receives a temporary, unguessable phone-hosted media URL and playback-control commands over the local network. It does not receive your Jellyfin URL, password, access token, or Authorization header.

3

Apps you choose through Android Share receive a diagnostic report only when you deliberately select Share and then choose a destination.

✓

No sale or advertising use. Stream Ferry does not sell, rent, monetize, or use your information for advertising or behavioral profiling.

04

Storage, retention, and deletion

App data is stored in Stream Ferry’s private Android storage. The Jellyfin server address and access token are encrypted with keys protected by Android Keystore. App backup is disabled.

  • Passwords: never stored.
  • Live stream buffers: bounded in memory and removed when the session ends.
  • Playback history: retained locally for up to 90 days unless you remove it sooner.
  • Offline downloads and cached metadata: retained until you delete the item, profile, or all app data.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics: locally capped and removable from Settings.

Your controls

You can remove one server profile, clear playback history or diagnostics, revoke local-file access in Android, delete individual downloads, or use Settings → Delete all app data. Uninstalling the app also removes its private data under Android’s normal uninstall behavior.

05

Android permissions

Stream Ferry asks only for permissions connected to playback and features you choose.

Network and Wi‑Fi stateConnect to your server, choose a safe local route, and keep an active stream available.
Local networkFind, connect to, and serve media to Cast or DLNA devices on your LAN.
NotificationsShow playback and download controls while a foreground service is active.
Video libraryOptional. Requested only if you choose “Allow all videos.” Android’s file and folder picker works without it.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, Bluetooth, accounts, SMS, call logs, or exact-alarm permissions.

06

This website

This Stream Ferry website contains no analytics, advertising pixels, contact forms, or third-party font requests. It stores only your light/dark theme choice in your browser’s local storage.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to provide and secure its hosting service. That processing is governed by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

07

Security

Remote Jellyfin servers must use HTTPS. Plain HTTP is accepted only for a server you explicitly approve on a trusted local network. Stream Ferry does not disable TLS certificate or hostname validation.

The TV-facing stream uses a temporary high-entropy session address bound to the active local-network session. Plain HTTP is used on this local hop because most TVs cannot trust a phone-generated certificate. Anyone who can monitor your LAN may still be able to observe streamed media bytes, so use Stream Ferry only on a network you trust.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Stream Ferry limits exposure by keeping tokens out of TV requests, rejecting unknown or expired stream paths, redacting diagnostics, disabling app backup, and closing the phone proxy when playback stops.

08

Children’s privacy

Stream Ferry is a general-purpose media utility and is not directed to children. Because the developer does not operate an account or data-collection service, Stream Ferry does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
