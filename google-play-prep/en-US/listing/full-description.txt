Your phone is the bridge.

Stream Ferry sends videos from Jellyfin or your Android device to a compatible TV while your phone stays in control. Choose a source, pick a screen on your network, then manage playback from the app.

BUILT FOR THE LIVING ROOM

• Browse Jellyfin libraries and local videos in one focused Android app.
• Discover Cast and DLNA/UPnP playback devices on your local network.
• Keep play, pause, seek, volume, and queue controls close at hand.
• Let the phone relay or transcode media when a TV cannot play the source directly.
• Save selected media for offline use on the phone.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Jellyfin server details and credentials stay on your phone. Stream Ferry has no ads, developer-operated analytics service, account system, or developer cloud backend. Local crash reports remain on the device unless you explicitly share them.

The Google Cast SDK sends anonymous Cast interaction, device, and app metadata to Google for aggregate SDK improvement. See the privacy policy for details.

WHAT YOU NEED

• Android 14 or newer.
• For Jellyfin: access to your own Jellyfin server.
• For TV playback: a compatible Cast or DLNA/UPnP device on the same network.

Compatibility depends on the TV, receiver, network, media format, and server configuration. Local-only playback works without a Jellyfin account.

Stream Ferry is source-available and free for personal use. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Jellyfin, Google, or TV manufacturers.
